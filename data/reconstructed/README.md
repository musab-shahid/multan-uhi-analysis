# Reconstructed outputs: provenance and limitations

These four CSVs were **uploaded by the researcher** after running a prior Colab reconstruction. Their contents were copied without recalculation. They are **not raw Landsat files** and **not independently verified measurements**.

| File | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| `annual_index_summary.csv` | Seven-year AOI means and standard deviations for surface temperature and spectral indices |
| `kmeans_2023_cluster_summary.csv` | Four exploratory clusters and their summary statistics; **historical label names are unvalidated** |
| `new_vulnerability_2023_areas.csv` | NEW reconstructed rule using 75th-percentile LST, 25th-percentile NDVI, 75th-percentile NDBI |
| `historical_vs_new_comparison.csv` | Difference from FYP's historical published percentages; **not a comparison of the same model** |

Refer to [execution verification](../../docs/RECONSTRUCTED_EXECUTION_VERIFICATION.md) and the preserved historical report. No claims of direct human heat exposure, causal attribution or independently validated land-cover classification.
